Ants vs humans: who solves problems better?

Imagine this: a T-shaped object, a maze, and two challengers, humans and ants.

In an intriguing experiment, humans were stripped of communication to mimic the limited interaction ants have. The task: manoeuvre the object through the maze.

  • Ants excelled, with strategic, coordinated teamwork, moving like a single unified entity toward a shared goal.
  • Humans struggled, with individuals focusing on their own short-term ideas, leading to inefficiency and a lack of cooperation.

The takeaway: collective intelligence and alignment toward a common purpose can outsmart even the smartest individuals working in isolation. Great things happen when we move together with a shared vision.
