How loud would the Sun be?

Ever wondered what the Sun would sound like if space weren't soundproof?

If space were filled with air, we'd hear the Sun at over 100 decibels, as loud as a chainsaw from five metres away. The energy it produces is like ten million piano keys playing at once.

With the Sun putting out an astounding 383 yottawatts (a million billion billion watts), we would be surrounded by a roar of around 290 decibels, far louder than any noise we can imagine.

Thankfully, space is silent. But it's fascinating to think about how powerful, and how noisy, our nearest star really is.
