Fashion's climate wake-up call

11 June 2025First posted on LinkedIn

Having worked in the fashion supply chain, I can say this report reflects the ground reality of brands' climate commitments.

The 2025 Fossil Free Fashion Scorecard evaluates fashion brands and retailers on their climate and energy targets. H&M Group took the top spot, setting a benchmark with ambitious Scope 3 targets (crucial, since over 80% of fashion's climate impact often comes from the supply chain) and strong transparency in energy reporting.

Kering is also making solid progress. It's concerning to see names like Abercrombie & Fitch, SHEIN and Columbia lagging with failing grades for a lack of meaningful climate action and transparency.

The stark reality: only a quarter of brands are stepping up with strong, 1.5°C-aligned targets for their supply chains. That underscores the urgent need to accelerate the race to net zero.

The takeaway: the industry needs bolder commitments, clearer transparency, and rapid action on renewable energy across the entire supply chain.
