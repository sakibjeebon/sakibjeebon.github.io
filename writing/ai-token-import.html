How many AI tokens does Bangladesh import?

28 March 2026First posted on LinkedIn

Here's a question nobody in Bangladesh is asking, but everyone should be: how many AI tokens does Bangladesh need to import every single year?

“Import tokens?” Yes. Every time you use OpenAI, Claude, GLM, Kimi or MiniMax, you're buying tokens from abroad. That's foreign currency walking out the door. Let's do the math.

The import bill, for 200,000 daily users

  • 20,000 tokens per user per day
  • About 4 billion tokens a day
  • About 1.46 trillion tokens a year
  • At $1 per million tokens, $1.46 million a year flowing out
  • Roughly ৳16 crore annually, just for 200,000 users

Now scale that to universities, hospitals, startups and government. The number becomes staggering.

What if we built it locally?

NVIDIA's Blackwell generation is a generational leap in inference performance. The B200 GPU delivers about 12,800 tokens per second per GPU in real-world production inference (MLPerf v5.1 benchmark on 70B-class models). On optimised stacks like TensorRT-LLM, it pushes up to 60,000 tokens per second per GPU.

Our demand? About 46,300 tokens per second to serve 200,000 daily users.

  • Just 4 × B200 GPUs cover the entire load (conservative estimate)
  • 1 × DGX B200 system (8 GPUs) handles it with full redundancy and room to scale
  • Hardware cost: about $240,000–$300,000
  • Year 1 total (capex, engineering team, electricity, liquid cooling): about $700K–$900K
  • Year 2 onwards: zero GPU capex, and costs drop sharply

Breakeven? Well within year 1. NVIDIA's own data shows a $5M investment in a Blackwell system can generate $75M in token revenue, a 15× return.

Bangladesh doesn't need to build a frontier model. We don't need our own GPT. We just need to serve our own demand locally, affordably and sovereignly. A country of 170 million people, with millions of students, researchers and professionals, all currently dependent on foreign AI infrastructure.

With about ৳8–10 crore in smart investment, someone could run a profitable token business while keeping Bangladesh's AI demand within its own borders. Imagine the token demand from our universities alone. Imagine the scale at the national level.

The question isn't “can we?” It's “why hasn't anyone started yet?”
