What a candle flame does in space

Ever wondered what happens to a candle flame in space? It's stranger than you'd think.

On Earth, candle flames are teardrop-shaped and bright yellow, thanks to glowing soot. In zero gravity they become blue, spherical and cooler.

Why? Without gravity, hot gases don't rise, so instead of convection, slow molecular diffusion takes over. That creates a gentle, calm, soot-free burn, and a blue glow instead of the usual yellow.
