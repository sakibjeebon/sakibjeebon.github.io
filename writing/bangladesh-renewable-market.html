Bangladesh opens its renewable power market

19 October 2025First posted on LinkedIn

A bold move: the Government of Bangladesh has launched the Policy on Commercial Power Generation/Purchase from Renewable Energy with Private Participation 2025, opening the door for private companies to build and operate merchant power plants.

Key highlights

  • Open market access: private firms can now sell clean electricity directly to consumers or the grid
  • End of monopoly: it breaks BPDB's single-buyer model and brings in competition
  • Investor-friendly: it welcomes local and global renewable energy investment
  • Greener future: it accelerates Bangladesh's 2030 target of 20% renewable power

This policy marks a structural shift in Bangladesh's power sector, from a centralised model to a more open, market-driven renewable ecosystem.
