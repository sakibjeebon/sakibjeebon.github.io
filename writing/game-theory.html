What game theory teaches about cooperation

I came across a video titled “What Game Theory Reveals About Life, The Universe, and Everything,” and the lessons are worth sharing.

Take the prisoner's dilemma: it shows how acting selfishly can trap us in bad outcomes. The twist is that cooperation isn't just nice; it's smart.

  • Be nice: don't start conflicts.
  • Be clear: transparency builds trust.
  • Be forgiving: grudges don't pay.
  • But don't be a pushover: retaliate fairly when needed.

The star of the show is Tit for Tat, a simple strategy that thrives by balancing kindness and fairness. In life, most wins come from working with others, not against them. Life isn't a zero-sum game; it's full of win-win opportunities if we play it right.
